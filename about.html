About Vajra

Building a Smarter EV Charging Future

Vajra is a community-powered EV charging platform that connects EV owners with trusted charger hosts while preparing for smart charger management and battery intelligence.

What We Are Building

Vajra combines accessible charging, intelligent management and future-ready battery insights.

⚡

Accessible Charging

Helping EV owners discover and reserve trusted charging locations more easily.

📊

Smart Management

Enabling hosts to monitor charger usage, energy delivery, bookings and earnings.

🔋

Battery Intelligence

Future insights for battery condition, charging behaviour, temperature and performance.
